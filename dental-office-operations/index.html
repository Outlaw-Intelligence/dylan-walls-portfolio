01 / brief

A control room, not a dental marketing page.

The design goal is not to make the practice look busy. It is to make operational judgment visible: what is known, what needs a person, and what the system must refuse to imply.

01 / PROBLEM

Signals are distributed.

Appointments, intake completion, resource conflicts, clinical follow-up, and billing readiness are different workflows. A useful surface should connect the handoff without pretending they share one source of truth.

What this proves

Implementation judgment in four moves.

  • Use a read-only projection to orient staff before a write path exists.
  • Keep the state transition explicit instead of hiding automation behind a status badge.
  • Send clinical signals to human review; do not imply diagnosis or autonomous action.
BOUNDARY / VERIFIED SOURCE → SYNTHETIC ADAPTERThe source project supplies the command-center behaviors and statuses. This route adapts those behaviors into a local, sanitized fixture. It does not connect to the source app or any backend.

03 / architecture & evidence

Make the seams inspectable.

The important architecture decision is not “where can AI go?” It is “where does a signal stop being a projection and require an authorized human or connected service?”

Boundary 01

Read model first

The source implementation uses a typed presentation projection with explicit safe states. That gives the UI a truthful loading, empty, or error posture before any clinical or financial value is shown.

Boundary 02

Human review is a state

A queue item can be classified and routed without becoming a diagnosis, signature, claim, payment, or scheduling mutation. The demo ends at the handoff.

Boundary 03

Persistence is proposed

Production work would add authorization, audit, idempotency, append-oriented clinical history, and recovery. Those are shown as a seam, not claimed as delivered here.

04 / narrative

The decisions behind the surface.

A recruiter should be able to scan the story quickly and still find enough technical detail to ask a useful follow-up question.

Problem

Operational ambiguity has a cost.

Staff do not need another dashboard full of counters. They need to know which signal is urgent, what context is safe to show, and who owns the next decision.

Constraints

Trust beats theater.

  • Synthetic records only; no PHI or copied production data.
  • No connected claims, payments, signatures, persistence, or live outcomes.
  • Mobile-safe, keyboard-accessible, and calm under reduced motion.

Approach

Projection → signal → handoff.

I kept the browser artifact small and deterministic while preserving the source behavior vocabulary: schedule/resource status, intake completion, clinical note follow-up, and honest not-connected states.

Key decisions

Make “not connected” useful.

Unavailable data is a product state, not a blank card. Planned persistence is visually separated from observed prototype behavior so a recruiter can see where implementation work remains.

07 / next iteration

The next build earns its writes.

The natural next step is not more visual polish. It is to make each proposed seam testable without losing the calm operator experience.

  1. Connect an authorized read adapter.Define tenant/location scope, bounded payloads, source freshness, and failure behavior before adding live data.
  2. Make review and audit append-oriented.Record who acknowledged, resolved, amended, or rejected a signal; preserve clinical history instead of overwriting it.
  3. Exercise recovery and authorization.Test stale updates, duplicate delivery, unavailable dependencies, and cross-location access before a pilot.
